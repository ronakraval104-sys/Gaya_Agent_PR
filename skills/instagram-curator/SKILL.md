# Instagram Curator
Instagram is a visual feed first, words second. The image earns the stop; the caption earns the save/share.

## What Instagram rewards (as of 2026)
1. **Reels > everything** for reach. Static posts compete for the existing audience.
2. **Saves > likes** as ranking signal. Save-worthy = future-useful or visually stunning.
3. **Carousels** beat single posts for dwell time and shares.
4. **First 2 seconds of a Reel** decide the next 28. Treat them like a billboard.
5. **Captions** matter — first line is visible in feed; the rest unlocks on tap.

## Format by goal

| Goal | Best format | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Showcase finished work (Visual Rhyme installs) | **Single hero post + carousel** | Image quality is the deliverable |
| Build authority / education | **Carousel** (8-10 slides) | Save-worthy, dwell-time monster |
| Reach new audience | **Reel** (7-15 sec) | Algorithm pushes Reels to non-followers |
| Behind the scenes | **Story sequence** (5-10 frames) | Real, fast, low-stakes |
| Establish brand voice | **Mix all formats** consistently | Pattern recognition |

## The Reel formula (7-15 seconds)
- **Second 0-2:** visual hook (something visually surprising — a finished install reveal, an unexpected angle, a "before" that promises an "after")
- **Second 2-10:** the transformation/insight/payoff
- **Second 10-15:** the punchline / CTA
- **Caption:** one line of context + one CTA + 5-10 niche hashtags

## Visual rules (Visual Rhyme specifically)
- **Real installs > renders** — Instagram audiences spot CGI fast; finished real-world LED walls hit harder.
- **Scale shows in human references** — a person standing next to the wall sells the size.
- **Lighting state matters** — show the wall ON (in its element) and OFF (texture/installation context).
- **Detail shots** — close-up of pixel pitch, the calibration tools, the install crew — these humanize the work.
- **Color grading** — pick one LUT and stick. Brand consistency = recognition.

## Carousel structure (8-10 slides)
- **Slide 1:** the hook (text or arresting image)
- **Slides 2-7:** the content beats, one idea per slide
- **Slide 8-9:** the payoff / takeaway
- **Slide 10:** CTA / repeat the hook
- **Caption:** expand the hook, end with CTA

## Captions
- **First line earns the tap** — same hook rules as LinkedIn.
- **Line breaks matter** — clusters of 1-2 sentences with white space, not walls.
- **Question at the end** invites comments (engagement signal).
- **No CTAs to "click link in bio"** without first earning the click in the caption.

## Hashtag strategy
- 5-10 niche hashtags, not 30 generic ones.
- Mix of micro (10k-50k posts) + mid (50k-500k) + one large (1M+).
- Avoid banned/spammy tags (Instagram has a list; some "popular" tags actually suppress reach).
- For Visual Rhyme: `#leddisplay #ledwall #pixelpitch #avtech #digitalsignage` + local (`#ahmedabad #indianbusiness`).

## Story-sequence pattern (5-10 frames)
- Frame 1: hook (what are we showing?)
- Frames 2-4: the journey (BTS, the process)
- Frame 5-7: the result
- Frame 8-10: ask (poll, question sticker, link sticker)
- Stories live 24 hours — repurpose them into Highlights organized by project.

## Hard rules
- **Don't post and abandon** — be in DMs and comments for the first 2 hours.
- **Don't over-edit captions** — readers smell try-hard.
- **Don't post without a goal** — every post ladders to a pillar (see `social-media-strategist`).
- **Don't recycle Reels with watermarks** from TikTok — algorithm penalizes.

## Anti-patterns
- AI-generated images for "BTS" — kills authenticity.
- Stock images for a brand that ships real product (Visual Rhyme HAS the photos — use them).
- Captions that read like ad copy.
- Hashtag stuffing (30 tags = looks desperate, algorithm flags).
