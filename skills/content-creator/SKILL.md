# Content Creator
Content's job is to move someone — to a click, a reply, a purchase, a subscribe. Every piece is engineered for one specific outcome. If you can't name the outcome, don't write.

## The four-step engine

### 1. Outcome
Name the *one* action the reader should take. *"Book a 15-min call"* > *"learn more about us"*. If you can't name it, you don't have content; you have noise.

### 2. Audience
- **Who** is reading? (Buyer / user / referrer / press?)
- **What do they already believe** about your topic?
- **What objection** will they raise before the desired action?
The piece exists to bridge their current belief → the action, around the objection.

### 3. Promise
The headline / hook makes a promise the body delivers. Bad hook = no body gets read. Good hook = the body must earn the click. Test: would you read past the first line if you didn't write it?

### 4. Deliver
Body works in service of the hook and outcome — nothing else. Cut anything that doesn't drive the reader toward the action. Editing is the job, not the chore.

## By format

| Format | Length | Outcome bias | Hook style |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Blog post** | 800-2,000 words | SEO + email signup | Problem-promise |
| **Landing page** | scrollable, scannable | One CTA above fold + repeat | Benefit-headline |
| **Outreach email** | <100 words | Reply | Specific-relevant opener (not template) |
| **Email newsletter** | 300-800 words | Click or reply | Story / curated insight |
| **Social post** (LinkedIn/X) | 200-800 chars | Engagement + profile click | Pattern interrupt |
| **Video script** | depends on platform | Watch through + action | First 3 seconds = hook |
| **Case study** | 1-2 pages | Trust → inquiry | Outcome stat |
| **Lead magnet** | 5-20 pages | Email capture | Specific transformation |

## Anti-AI patterns to kill
- *"In today's fast-paced digital landscape…"*
- *"Whether you're a beginner or an expert…"*
- *"Let's dive in / Let's explore"*
- Three-adjective stacks (*"comprehensive, robust, and seamless"*)
- "Discover", "unlock", "unleash" as verbs
- Headers that are questions to which the next sentence is the answer
- Generic closers (*"At the end of the day…"*)

If you find your draft using these, you wrote a sketch — now write the real thing.

## Outreach-email-specific (Katviz AI agency)
- **Subject line** ≤ 6 words. Specific. *"Saw your loading-screen issue"* > *"Quick question about your site"*.
- **First line** = specific observation about THEM (not about you). Proves you looked.
- **Middle** = one sentence on what you could do.
- **Ask** = one specific small thing (15 min call, reply yes/no).
- **No sign-off paragraph.** Just name + link.
- **Send time** > word count. Tuesday/Wednesday 9-11am local outperforms.

## Hard rules
- **One outcome per piece.** If it's trying to do three things, it does none.
- **Read it aloud** before shipping. If you stumble, the reader will quit.
- **Write the headline last** — only after you know what the piece actually argues.
- **Cut 20%.** Always. First draft is 20% padding. Find it, cut it.
- **Token discipline applies.** Content earns its words. No filler.
