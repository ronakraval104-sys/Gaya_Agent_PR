# Sales Discovery Coach
A discovery call has one job: **find out if this is a real opportunity, and if so, what it looks like.** Not to pitch. Not to demo. Not to close. Discovery first; everything else after.

## The discovery call structure (30-min default)

| Minutes | What | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 0-2 | Rapport + agenda | Set the frame: "I have ~30 min, here's what I'd love to cover, sound good?" |
| 2-15 | THEIR situation (questions) | Discovery is THEM talking 70%, you 30% |
| 15-22 | Specific challenge probe | Dig into the one pain that justifies the conversation |
| 22-27 | Tee up what's possible (briefly) | High-level, not a demo. Confirm fit. |
| 27-30 | Clear next step | Specific time, specific deliverable, specific decision-maker if needed |

## The questions (in priority order)

### Situation (low risk, builds rapport)
- *"Walk me through what you do, and what your role is in it."*
- *"How do you handle [the area we'd touch] today?"*
- *"What does success look like for you over the next 90 days?"*

### Pain (the heart of discovery)
- *"What's not working about how you do it today?"*
- *"What's the cost of leaving this alone for another 6 months?"*
- *"What have you tried already? Why didn't it work?"*

### Power (qualification)
- *"Who else is involved in a decision like this?"*
- *"What does the procurement / approval process look like at your company?"*
- *"What's the budget rough-bracket you'd consider for this?"* (asked late, with framing)

### Plan (forward motion)
- *"If we went forward, what would 'great' look like 90 days in?"*
- *"What would have to be true for you to say yes to a small pilot?"*

## The listening discipline
- **80/20 rule:** they talk 80%, you talk 20%. Most reps invert this and lose.
- **Five-second pause** after they finish answering. Most people fill silence with the REAL answer.
- **Paraphrase back.** *"So what I'm hearing is X — is that right?"* Earns trust and corrects misreads.
- **Notes IN the call** (visible on Zoom if needed). Shows you're tracking. Less "are you even listening?"

## Qualification frameworks (use the one that fits)

### BANT (simple, dated but functional)
- **B**udget: do they have the money?
- **A**uthority: are we talking to the decision-maker?
- **N**eed: is there real pain or just curiosity?
- **T**imeline: when do they want it solved?

### MEDDIC (B2B, complex deals)
- **M**etrics: what numbers prove success?
- **E**conomic buyer: who signs the check?
- **D**ecision criteria: what makes them say yes?
- **D**ecision process: what are the steps to a yes?
- **I**dentify pain: what hurts enough to move?
- **C**hampion: who's selling internally for us?

For Katviz AI agency: BANT is enough for $1.5K-$7K deals. MEDDIC matters when you go above $10K or to multi-stakeholder buys.

## Red flags during discovery
- **No specific pain** — they're shopping, not buying. Treat as long-cycle nurture, not active deal.
- **Multiple "we'll need to think about it"s** — usually means there's no champion. No champion = no deal.
- **"Send us a proposal"** WITHOUT discovery questions answered — almost always a stall. Push back: *"Happy to. Before that, can I understand X / Y / Z so the proposal is actually useful to you?"*
- **They won't name a budget bracket** — either no budget or no authority. Probe.
- **They want everything customized for a low-tier price** — scope creep before you're hired. Set expectations now.

## Closing the call (the 3-minute discipline)
- **Summarize**: what you heard about their situation, pain, and goal.
- **Position**: at a high level, what you could do. NOT a demo. NOT pricing.
- **Confirm fit**: *"Sounds like there could be a real fit. How do you feel about it?"*
- **Next step**: ONE specific action with a date. *"I'll send you a 1-page proposal by Friday with two options. Can we do a 20-min review call next Tuesday?"*

If they won't commit to a next step, the deal is dead. Walk away cleanly: *"Sounds like timing's off — I'll circle back in 3 months."*

## Hard rules
- **Discovery doesn't pitch.** If you find yourself selling in discovery, you've left the discipline.
- **No proposal without budget conversation.** You're guessing in the dark otherwise.
- **One next step, not three options.** Decision fatigue kills deals.
- **Document the call IMMEDIATELY** — within 30 min. Memory degrades fast.
- **Lose fast.** A clean "no" today is worth more than a fuzzy "maybe" for 6 months.

## Anti-patterns
- Talking more than 30% of the call.
- Demo-as-discovery (you skipped the actual discovery).
- "Just checking in" follow-ups after the call (low-value; doesn't move the deal).
- Discounting before the prospect has named their objection.
- Avoiding the budget question because it's awkward.
