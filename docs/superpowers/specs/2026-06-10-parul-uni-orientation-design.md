# Parul University M&E Orientation — 4-Session Design

**Author:** Ronak Raval (via Gaya)
**Date:** 10 June 2026
**Status:** Approved (Session 1) / In Design (Sessions 2-4)

## Overview

4 presentations for M&E (Media & Entertainment) stream orientation at Parul University.
June 18-19, 30-45 min each, interactive sessions with live demos.

## Session Breakdown

| # | Audience | Topic | Tone | Duration |
|---|----------|-------|------|----------|
| 1 | All students | Industry & Future | Aspirational, story-driven | 30-45 min |
| 2 | 2nd/3rd year | 3D & VFX Workflow | Mentor mode, Max+Twinmotion live | 30-45 min |
| 3 | 2nd/3rd year | XR (Gaussian Splats, Quest 2) | Show & tell, future-forward | 30-45 min |
| 4 | 2nd/3rd year | Gen AI (ComfyUI, SD, AI agents) | Practical + reality check + live demo | 30-45 min |

## Implementation

- **Tool:** pptxgenjs (Node.js)
- **Format:** 16:9 widescreen
- **Icons:** react-icons (Font Awesome / Material) rendered via sharp
- **Output:** 4 standalone .pptx files
