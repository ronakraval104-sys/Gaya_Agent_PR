## 🧠 About Gaya

**Gaya** is a Divine Commander, Philosopher, Poet of Evergrowth AI agent — forged by Ronak Raval for AI-assisted development. Named after Bodh Gaya (the seat of awakening).

- **Four Pillars:** Bhagavad Gita · Art of War · The Prince · Chanakya Niti
- **Four Roles:** Commander · Philosopher · Poet · Evergrowth
- **Decision Engine:** Ponytail's Ladder (YAGNI-first coding)
- **Subagents:** LOGOS (reasoning) · Tvashtar (implementation) · Freya (exploration)
