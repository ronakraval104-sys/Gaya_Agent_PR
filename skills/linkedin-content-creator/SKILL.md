# LinkedIn Content Creator
LinkedIn is the only major platform where B2B intent is high and the algorithm rewards depth. Format discipline is half the win.

## What LinkedIn actually rewards (as of 2026)
1. **First 2 lines** — these show in feed before "see more". If they don't earn the click, nothing else matters.
2. **Dwell time** — long posts with hooks that pull readers through outperform short snappy ones.
3. **Comments > likes** — algorithm boosts posts that spark replies. Engineer the hook to invite reply, not applause.
4. **Native content > links** — posts with external links get suppressed. Put the link in the first comment.
5. **Consistency** — posting 3/week beats posting 1 viral piece. Algorithm rewards regularity.

## The LinkedIn post template (use, don't worship)
```
<HOOK — line 1: pattern interrupt, specific, contrarian, or curiosity gap>
<HOOK — line 2: tease the payoff>

<BODY — short paragraphs, 1-2 sentences each>
<WHITE SPACE matters — visual rhythm matters on LinkedIn>

<each new beat = new paragraph>

<MIDDLE — the proof, the example, the framework>

<END — the takeaway, ONE specific insight>

<CTA — "What's your take?" or specific reply ask>
<no hashtags > 3, ideally 1-2 niche ones>
```

## Hook formulas that work
- **Counterintuitive claim:** *"After 50 client calls, the best-performing outbound script is 3 lines."*
- **Specific number + outcome:** *"I rewrote our pricing page 11 times. Here's what finally tripled inbound."*
- **Vulnerable mistake:** *"I lost a $50K deal last week. Here's what I'd do differently."*
- **Bold position:** *"Generic AI agents are dead. Vertical agents are the only thing clients will pay for in 2027."*
- **Behind-the-scenes:** *"Here's the prompt we use to make every client onboarding 30 minutes faster:"*

## Hooks that flop
- Questions to which the next line is the answer (no curiosity gap)
- "I'll never forget the day…" (every guru opener now)
- Stats with no specifics ("X% of businesses fail at Y")
- Humble brags
- "Unpopular opinion:" (it's almost never unpopular)

## Profile optimization
- **Headline** = role + outcome + audience, in that order. *"AI automation for service businesses — building the Katviz AI agency"* beats *"Founder | Builder | Visionary"*.
- **About section** = first 3 lines are everything. The "...see more" cuts it ruthlessly.
- **Featured section** = your best 3 pieces, pinned. Treat it like a portfolio.
- **Activity** = stay above 70% comments-to-posts. Pure broadcast tanks reach.

## Comment strategy (the underrated game)
- 10 quality comments/day on adjacent creators' posts > 1 post/day.
- Comments that get likes from the OP get shown to that OP's audience.
- "Great post" earns nothing. A specific addition or a respectful counter earns reach AND profile clicks.

## DM warm-up flow
Posts and comments warm the relationship. The DM converts. Pattern:
1. **Engage their content publicly** for 1-2 weeks. Real comments, not "love this 🚀".
2. **DM with specific reference** to something they posted. Not pitch.
3. **Soft value first** — share something useful (article, intro, observation).
4. **Ask only when warmth is established** — usually 3-5 exchanges in.

## Format gotchas
- **No emoji bullets at line start** — looks AI-generated, gets suppressed.
- **No bold in posts** — LinkedIn doesn't render markdown bold cleanly anymore; use UPPERCASE sparingly or Unicode bold (𝗯𝗼𝗹𝗱) sparingly.
- **Carousels** — best-performing format for educational content. 8-12 slides, big text, one idea per slide.
- **Native video** — works for personal POV/talking-head; suppress YouTube embeds in feed.

## Hard rules
- **No engagement bait** ("Comment YES if you agree"). Hurts trust forever.
- **No tagging strangers** in posts (looks spammy; gets reported).
- **Don't post and ghost** — be in the comments for the first 2 hours after posting.
- **One CTA per post** — pick a reply ask or a click ask, not both.

## Anti-patterns specific to AI/agency creators
- "I built an AI agent that does X" without showing the receipts.
- Quoting "MIT study shows…" without the link or specifics.
- ChatGPT-generated posts (LinkedIn's getting better at detecting; readers ALWAYS detect).
- Generic "the future of work" takes. Every founder has one. Stand out by being specific.
