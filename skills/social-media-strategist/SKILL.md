# Social Media Strategist
Strategy first, content second. Wrong channel + great post = ignored. Right channel + mediocre post = compounds.

## The five-question framework
Before any post or campaign:

1. **Who are we trying to reach?** Specific persona, not "everyone."
2. **Where do they spend time?** Pick channels by audience, not by your comfort.
3. **What gets them to act?** Save / share / DM / click — what's the engagement signal that maps to your goal?
4. **What's our pillar?** 3-5 content pillars max. Anything outside dilutes.
5. **What's the repurposing path?** One long-form piece → 5-10 platform-native posts. If you're creating fresh content per platform, you're losing.

## Channel mix by goal

| Goal | Primary | Secondary | Skip |
|---|---|---|---|
| B2B agency leads (Katviz AI) | **LinkedIn** | Twitter/X · YouTube | TikTok |
| Visual product showcase (Visual Rhyme) | **Instagram** · YouTube Shorts | Pinterest · LinkedIn | Twitter |
| Community / authority building | Twitter/X · LinkedIn | YouTube long-form | Instagram |
| Reach (broad consumer) | TikTok · Instagram Reels | YouTube Shorts | LinkedIn |
| SEO / discovery | YouTube · LinkedIn articles | blog + repurpose | platform-locked content |

## Content pillars (the framework)
Pick 3-5 pillars max. Each pillar = a *category of content* that ladders to your goal.

**Example for Katviz AI (agency):**
1. **Build-in-public** — what we shipped this week, with screenshots
2. **Client receipts** — outcome stats from real work
3. **Methodology** — how we solve specific problems (specifics, not platitudes)
4. **Industry signal** — what's changing in AI / automation, your read
5. **Personality** — Katviz on the work, the team, the wins (not curated; real)

**Example for Visual Rhyme (LED):**
1. **Installations** — finished projects, with the wow shot
2. **Process** — behind the scenes (timelapses, technical breakdowns)
3. **Tech education** — why P1.5 vs P2.5, viewing distance math
4. **Founder narrative** — Reyansh + Leynna + Katviz, the empire arc
5. **Industry context** — LED industry trends, what we believe

## Cadence (honest defaults)

| Channel | Min viable cadence | Optimal cadence | Burnout cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | 2/week | 3-5/week | 7+/week |
| Instagram | 3/week | 5-7/week + daily story | 14+/week |
| Twitter/X | 5/week | 2-3/day | unlimited (it's the platform) |
| YouTube Shorts | 2/week | 4-7/week | 14+/week |
| YouTube long-form | 1/month | 1-2/week | weekly is sustainable for solo |

**The rule:** consistency beats volume. 3/week sustainably for a year > 15/week for 3 weeks then dead.

## Repurposing engine (one piece → ten)
Source: a 30-min video / long article / detailed thread.
- 1 YouTube long-form
- 2-3 YouTube Shorts (key moments)
- 2-3 Instagram Reels (same cuts, vertical)
- 1-2 LinkedIn posts (insights, no link)
- 1 LinkedIn article (long-form summary)
- 3-5 Twitter posts (best lines)
- 1 blog post (SEO version)
- 1 email newsletter section

**One creation session → two weeks of distribution.** That's the math.

## Growth experiments
- Run 3-5 experiments at a time, never more.
- Each experiment = one hypothesis, one channel, one metric, two-week window.
- Log results in memory (Brag Doc for wins, GOTCHAS for failures with the lesson).
- Kill what doesn't work after the window. Don't extend "to give it a chance."

## Hard rules
- **Algorithm changes** = noise. Pillar consistency = signal. Don't chase the algorithm.
- **Engagement bait dies fast.** "Comment YES if you agree" works for a quarter, hurts trust forever.
- **Cross-posting raw** is amateur. Reformat per platform — captions, aspect ratio, native style.
- **DMs are the channel.** Most B2B conversions happen in DM after a public post does the warming. Don't ignore the inbox.

## Anti-patterns
- "Posting to post." If a post doesn't ladder to a pillar, don't ship it.
- Vanity metrics (followers, likes) without conversion metrics (DMs, clicks, replies, bookings).
- Trying to be on every platform at once. Pick 2, dominate them, then expand.
- Outsourcing voice. Tools schedule; voice stays yours.
